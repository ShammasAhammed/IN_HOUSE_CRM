# Moving a client off WhatsApp: the client chat

Each client can have a chat shared with the people you invite to their portal. It sits next to the team's own
client chat, which clients never see. In Oppllo One it's the chat marked **Client sees** (the client page's
**Chat with client** button); for the client it's **Messages** in their portal.

## What the client sees
- Only their own chat: never the team's chat, other clients, Oppllo's own spaces, hours, fees or AI costs.
- Links into Oppllo One work only when the portal can open them (a creative waiting for them, a final report, one
  of their requests). Any other link shows as "internal link".
- They write as themselves, can change a message for 15 minutes and remove it any time ("Message removed").
- Removing their portal access, or the contact, closes the chat at once.

## What the team sees
- **Clients waiting for a reply** at the top of Chat, oldest first, with how long they've waited.
- A **Client can see this** banner in the shared chat. Before a message with hours, fees, rupee amounts or another
  client's name goes out, Oppllo One asks **Send to the client?** (you can still send it).
- Only the client's team writes there (the owner, the lead strategist, and members from "comment" level); others
  who see the client can read. **@AI** in this chat answers only you and is never posted.
- **Draft reply** follows the client's tone of voice; Malayalam goes to Gemini or ChatGPT, never Claude.
- The AI co-worker suggests a teammate answer when a client has waited a working day.
- The team gets a notification when a client writes. The client gets an email "You have a new message from Oppllo"
  at most once an hour while they haven't read it, only once email sending is set up.

## The move-over, per client
The client page's portal card has a checklist (invited → signed in → first message → WhatsApp group closed, the last
one is yours to do) and these messages to copy. Change the date before sending.

**Invite message (English):** Hello! From now on you can see <client>'s results, approve our creatives, send
requests and message the Oppllo team in one place: the Oppllo client portal. Please look for the email from Oppllo
One with your sign-in link (it works once). Thank you!

**Invite message (Malayalam):** നമസ്കാരം! ഇനി മുതൽ <client>-ന്റെ ഫലങ്ങൾ കാണാനും ഞങ്ങളുടെ ക്രിയേറ്റീവുകൾ
അംഗീകരിക്കാനും ആവശ്യങ്ങൾ അറിയിക്കാനും Oppllo ടീമിന് മെസ്സേജ് അയക്കാനും ഒരൊറ്റ ഇടം: Oppllo ക്ലയന്റ് പോർട്ടൽ.
സൈൻ ഇൻ ലിങ്കുള്ള Oppllo One ഇമെയിൽ നോക്കുക (ലിങ്ക് ഒരു തവണ മാത്രം പ്രവർത്തിക്കും). നന്ദി!

**For the WhatsApp group (English):** Hello everyone! From <date>, please message us in the Oppllo client portal
(Messages) instead of this group. Approvals, files and requests stay together there and nothing gets lost. Your
sign-in link is in your email. We'll close this group after <date>. Thank you!

**For the WhatsApp group (Malayalam):** എല്ലാവർക്കും നമസ്കാരം! <date> മുതൽ ഈ ഗ്രൂപ്പിന് പകരം Oppllo ക്ലയന്റ്
പോർട്ടലിലെ Messages-ൽ ഞങ്ങൾക്ക് മെസ്സേജ് അയക്കുക. അംഗീകാരങ്ങളും ഫയലുകളും ആവശ്യങ്ങളും അവിടെ ഒരുമിച്ച്
ഉണ്ടാകും, ഒന്നും നഷ്ടപ്പെടില്ല. സൈൻ ഇൻ ലിങ്ക് നിങ്ങളുടെ ഇമെയിലിലുണ്ട്. <date>-ന് ശേഷം ഈ ഗ്രൂപ്പ് ക്ലോസ്
ചെയ്യും. നന്ദി!

Have a Malayalam speaker read the Malayalam texts once before the first client gets them.
