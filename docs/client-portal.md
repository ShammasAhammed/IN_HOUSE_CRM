# Client portal: a guide for the owner

The client portal lets the people at a college or school see their own work with Oppllo without asking: this month's
results, creatives waiting for their approval, a way to send requests, and the monthly report once it's final.

## How to invite someone

1. Open the client in Oppllo One. Make sure the contact (say, the principal) is listed with their **email**.
2. In the **Client portal** card, press **Invite** next to them. Only the owner and strategists can do this.
3. **If email sending is set up**, they get an email with a sign-in link.
   **If it isn't set up yet** (no Resend key), nothing is emailed: you see the message and a **Copy link** button.
   Send them that link yourself (WhatsApp or email). It works **once, within 7 days**.
4. They open the link, press **Sign in**, read and accept the short terms (kept as their consent under DPDP), and
   they're in. Next time they can use **Email me a sign-in link** on the portal's sign-in page, or **Continue with
   Google** if their Google account uses the invited email. There are no passwords.

The portal's address is `/portal` on the Oppllo One site (for example `https://oppllo-one.vercel.app/portal`).

## What clients see

- **Home**: this month's leads, admissions and counselling calls against target, cost per lead, and how the month is
  going (On track / At risk / Off track, with the reason). What was delivered this month (reels, posts, creatives
  approved) and what's coming up on the calendar.
- **Creatives**: anything you've sent "to the client" for approval. They can open it, comment, **Approve**, or **Ask
  for changes** with a note. Their decision is recorded as theirs, with their name and the time; you no longer need
  to record it on their behalf.
- **Requests**: a short form. Each request becomes a task for the client's lead strategist, tagged **Request**.
- **Reports**: monthly reports once they're made **final**. Drafts never show.

## What clients never see

Hours, retainer budgets or burn, fees, invoices, internal notes, staff comments on creatives, other clients, Oppllo's
own spaces, people, HR, leads, AI costs. A portal sign-in can't open any staff page or download.

## Removing access

- Press **Remove access** in the Client portal card: it ends at once, even if they're signed in right now.
- Removing the contact from the client, or changing their email, also ends their access at once.
- To let them back in, invite them again.

## Keeping track

Every invite, sign-in, approval, comment and request is recorded. You can see portal sign-ins in **Settings → Audit
log**.
